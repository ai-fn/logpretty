# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want logpretty to be a project where anyone can report a bug, ask a question or send a patch
and be treated with respect, whatever their experience, background, identity or the language
they write in.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient, especially with people who are new to Go, to the project or to open source.
- Criticize code, not people. Give the reason along with the objection.
- Assume good faith, and say so when you are unsure what someone meant.
- Accept review and feedback gracefully, and give it the same way.
- Stay on topic: an issue or a pull request is about logpretty.

## What we do not accept

- Harassment, insults, personal attacks, trolling or deliberately provoking others.
- Discriminatory language or jokes, and unwelcome sexual attention.
- Publishing someone's private information, such as an address or an email, without their permission.
- Pressing a maintainer or a contributor to answer, merge or change a decision.
- Anything else that would be out of place in a professional setting.

## Scope

This applies to every space the project runs: issues, pull requests, discussions, commit
messages and review comments. It also applies when someone speaks for the project elsewhere.

## Enforcement

Report a violation privately to the maintainer, [@ai-fn](https://github.com/ai-fn), through the
contact options on their GitHub profile. For a comment or an issue, you can also use GitHub's
"Report content". Reports are read by the maintainer only and are kept private. If the report is
about the maintainer, send it to GitHub Support through their abuse report form instead.

The maintainer may edit or delete comments, lock or close threads, and reject contributions that
break this code. For a repeated or serious violation they may block the person from the project,
temporarily or for good. Where it is possible, a first violation gets a private warning that
names what was wrong.
